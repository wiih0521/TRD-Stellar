\textbf{Matrix-tree theorem.} Build the Laplacian $L$: for every edge
$a\to b$, do $L_{ab}\mathrel{-}=1,\ L_{bb}\mathrel{+}=1$ (also the reverse
for an undirected edge). Delete row and column $r$; its determinant is the
number of directed spanning trees rooted at $r$.

\textbf{Erd\H{o}s--Gallai.} A non-increasing sequence
$d_1,\ldots,d_n$ is the degree sequence of a simple graph iff its sum is even
and, for every $1\le k\le n$,
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^{k}d_i\le k(k-1)+\sum_{i=k+1}^{n}\min(d_i,k).
\]
